# Concepts

- **Development eligible**: both task endpoints pass, paired micro gain is positive, and equal-family macro gain is positive.
- **Legacy retained**: development eligible with at least three of five perturbation families non-degraded within the frozen tolerance.
- **Unique-source quota**: no selected prompts share a construction source, and each of eight categories contributes at least three rows.
- **Development No-Go**: technically valid endpoints whose frozen selection gate fails; it is a scientific negative result, not a runtime failure.
- **Conditional experiment**: an experiment that must not run unless its registered parent hypothesis is supported.
