# H5 Paired Output-Mismatch Protocol (Preregistered 2026-08-08)

H3 and H4 remain immutable No-Go results. H3 compared independent stochastic seed banks and falsely flagged intact. H4 corrected intact specificity with common random numbers, but its signed mean-feature statistic missed all three Gaussian perturbations because seed-level effects could cancel in direction. H4 outcomes are used only to formulate H5; no H5 endpoint is reused from H4.

H5 reuses the exact G3 MCC12 artifact (SHA-256 `88946ec70c87a3b7f19e45652f277e50beaf1c7fbf5a7508b691e24370a8df09`) and freezes ten new common generation seeds (`2026081700`-`2026081709`). For each seed, the twelve target responses are compared byte-for-byte with the same-seed reference responses. A seed block is positive if any of its twelve responses differs. The primary one-sided exact binomial test uses the seed block as the independent unit, an operational intact block-mismatch null rate of 0.10, and per-state alpha `0.05/3 = 0.016666666666666666` to guard three intact reloads. With ten blocks, at least four positive blocks are required for rejection. Prompt-stratified and pooled MMD remain secondary diagnostics.

The endpoint set contains three new intact reloads and eleven newly seeded modified states: two unstructured-pruning, two structured-pruning, three Gaussian, two quantization, and two LoRA instances. H-H5 is supported only if all 14 states are technically valid, all 3 intact states remain unflagged, at least 9/11 modified states are detected, every modified family is represented among detections, and all 3/3 Gaussian states are detected. The Gaussian requirement is additional to, not a replacement for, the overall gate.

No H5 endpoint may change the MCC12, reference/target seeds, null rate, alpha, four-block rejection boundary, state definitions, Gaussian magnitudes, family requirements, response comparison, or secondary diagnostics. Negative results are recorded without repair. The claim is limited to the fixed model revision, runtime, prompts, seeds, and registered model-state instances; it is not a population detection-rate estimate.
